[Source record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Close_up_shot_of_the_human_eye,_9_August_2024.jpg) · [CC0 dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

The original file is retained unchanged. SHA-256 and download URL are in `source.json`. Apollo samples unobstructed sectors, reconstructs occluded sectors, normalizes colour and fits native eye UVs. The photographed flash reflections and pupil are excluded. Output dimensions do not imply additional photographic resolution.
